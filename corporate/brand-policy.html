Use of Open Insights models, data and brand

The models, code, data and assumptions behind Open Insights analysis are open-source and published through the M3 Platform. We encourage anyone to use them. Using them does not make the results an Open Insights product.

Only analysis published by Open Insights, through the Energy Policy Monitor or a Custom Scenario Analysis released jointly with our partners, can be labelled Open Insights analysis. Work by anyone else that uses the M3 Platform, its component models, or assumptions and data published by Open Insights is the sole responsibility of its authors. That holds even when it starts from an Open Insights scenario or input data. Open Insights has not reviewed or validated such work and does not endorse its findings.

When describing such work, you may say that it used the M3 Platform or a named component model (for example, COPPER or MacroABM-CA).

You may not:

  • attribute findings, results or conclusions to Open Insights or the Energy Policy Monitor (for example, “Open Insights modelling shows…” or “according to Open Insights models…”);
  • describe the work as produced, reviewed, validated or endorsed by Open Insights, the Energy Policy Monitor, or any of their partner organizations; or
  • use the Open Insights or Energy Policy Monitor names, logos or visual identity in any report, presentation, publication, website, media or promotional material.

If you would like to collaboratively produce or release analysis with Open Insights, please reach out to deven@openinsights.ca.

If you are citing a published Open Insights assessment, please cite it directly and do not present new or modified results as part of it. See the M3 documentation for how to cite and acknowledge the M3 Platform.
